# Privacy Policy for LiDARPeek

Last updated: November 6, 2026

LiDARPeek ("the App") is a mobile application that displays public LiDAR hillshade terrain data on a map.

## Data we do not collect

The App does not require an account. We do not collect, store, or transmit personal data to our servers. There is no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking.

## Location

The App can use your device location to center the map. Location data stays on your device and is not sent to us.

## Network requests

To display maps and terrain, the App requests tiles directly from public services (for example basemap providers and national LiDAR services such as GUGiK). These requests go from your device to those services. We do not proxy or log them.

## Payments

Purchases are handled by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment details.
